FReSH turns sparse, uncalibrated videos into metric-scale scenes and world-grounded hand motion with a feed-forward model.

Overview

FReSH jointly reconstructs articulated hands, scene geometry, and camera trajectories from one or a few uncalibrated video streams. Detailed hand observations and global scene context inform one another, while causal memory connects successive reconstructions in a shared metric world.
